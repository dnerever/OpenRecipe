## Brown everything

1. Sear the king oyster coins hard in oil until deeply caramelised on both cut
   faces. Lift them out.
2. Brown the cremini in batches. Crowding the pan steams them, and steamed
   mushrooms will not carry a braise this long.
3. Soften the carrot and onion in the fat, then add the garlic, tomato paste and
   smoked paprika and cook until the paste darkens.

## Braise

1. Return all the mushrooms. Dust with flour and toss to coat.
2. Add the wine and stock, thyme and bay. Bring to a bare simmer.
3. Cover and cook at 325°F for 90 minutes, until the liquid has thickened and
   the mushrooms have given up everything they have.

## Garnish

1. Brown the pearl onions separately in oil until blistered.
2. Fold them in for the last 20 minutes so they keep their shape.
3. Check the salt and rest it off the heat for 20 minutes before serving.

## Pastry

Rub the butter into the flour and icing sugar until it looks like coarse sand.
Bind with the yolk and water, flatten into a disc, and chill an hour.

Roll thin, line the tin, and chill again. Blind bake at 350°F for 20 minutes,
remove the beans, and bake 10 more until sandy and pale gold.

## Filling

Whisk the eggs and sugar without aerating them. Stir in the juice, zest and
cream, then rest 30 minutes and skim the foam.

Pour into the warm shell and bake at 250°F for 35-45 minutes, until it wobbles
as one piece. Cool completely before cutting.

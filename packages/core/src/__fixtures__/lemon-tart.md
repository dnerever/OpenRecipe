## Pastry

Rub the butter into the flour and icing sugar until it looks like coarse sand.
Bind with the ice water, flatten into a disc, and chill an hour.

Roll thin, line the tin, and chill again. Blind bake at 350°F for 20 minutes,
remove the beans, and bake 10 more until sandy and pale gold.

## Filling

Whisk the cornflour into a little of the coconut cream until smooth, then add
the rest with the sugar, juice, zest, aquafaba and turmeric.

Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until it thickens and just begins to
bubble — about 6 minutes. Keep whisking for one more minute off the heat.

Pour into the warm shell and smooth the top. Chill at least 4 hours; it sets as
it cools rather than in the oven, so do not be tempted to bake it further.

Steep the kombu and shiitake in the cold water for 30 minutes, then heat slowly
to just below a simmer and lift them out. Boiling the kombu turns the dashi
bitter and slick.

Rehydrate the wakame in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain.

Add the tofu and wakame to the warm dashi and heat through.

Slake the miso with a ladle of the hot dashi in a small bowl, then stir it back
in off the heat. Scatter the spring onion over and serve immediately.

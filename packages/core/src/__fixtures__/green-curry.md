Fry the tofu in a dry wok until golden on several sides, then set it aside. It
will hold its shape far better than tofu added raw to the sauce.

Spoon the thick cream off the top of the coconut milk into the wok over medium
heat. Fry it until the fat separates and it smells nutty.

Add the curry paste and fry another two minutes, until it darkens and the
kitchen fills with it.

Pour in the rest of the coconut milk, the eggplant, bamboo shoots, soy sauce and
palm sugar. Simmer 15 minutes, then return the tofu for the last five.

Off the heat, tear in the lime leaves and basil. Check the balance: it should be
salty first, then sweet, then hot.

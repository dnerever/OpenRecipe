## Levain

Mix the starter, flour and water in a small tub. Cover and rest 8 hours at 78°F,
until it floats in water.

## Autolyse

Combine both flours with 700g of the water. Mix until no dry flour remains, then
rest 40 minutes.

## Mix

Add the levain and squeeze it through the dough. Rest 30 minutes, then add the
salt with the remaining 50g water.

## Bulk ferment

Fold every 30 minutes for the first 3 hours, then rest until the dough has risen
about 30% and feels airy.

## Shape and proof

Pre-shape into rounds, bench rest 30 minutes, then shape tightly and place seam
up in a rice-floured banneton. Retard overnight at 39°F.

## Bake

Preheat the dutch oven to 500°F. Score, bake covered 20 minutes, uncover, drop to
450°F and bake 25-30 minutes more until deeply coloured.

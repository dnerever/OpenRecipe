Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then freeze it for an hour. Frozen
grains shatter and break down faster.

Bring the stock to a boil with the shiitake soaking liquid, add the rice, and
drop to the barest simmer. Stir every 15 minutes for 90 minutes, until the
grains have dissolved into cream.

Marinate the tofu batons in the soy sauce, sesame oil and a little white pepper.
Slide them in with the sliced shiitake during the last ten minutes.

Serve with the ginger and spring onion piled on top, and chilli oil for anyone
who wants it.

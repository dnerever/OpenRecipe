Dissolve the yeast in the water. Add the flour and mix to a shaggy mass, then
rest 20 minutes.

Add the salt and knead until the dough passes a windowpane test, about 10
minutes by hand.

Bulk ferment 2 hours at room temperature, then divide into 250g balls and
refrigerate 48 hours in a sealed box.

Take the balls out 3 hours before baking. Open them by hand, never with a pin.

## Soffritto

Sweat the pancetta until its fat runs, then add the onion, carrot and celery.
Cook gently for 20 minutes, until sweet and completely soft. Do not colour it.

## Brown the meat

Raise the heat and add both minces, breaking them up. Cook until the water has
driven off and the meat starts to fry rather than steam — 15 minutes at least.

## Milk

Pour in the milk and simmer until it has almost entirely evaporated. It will look
curdled and wrong. It is not.

## Wine, then tomato

Add the wine and reduce it away. Only then add the tomato, stock and nutmeg.

## The long part

Barely simmer, uncovered, for 4 hours, topping up with water if it tightens too
far. It is done when the fat separates orange at the edges.

## The day before

Pat the bird completely dry. Salt it inside and out, then leave it uncovered on a
rack in the fridge for 24 hours. The skin should feel like parchment.

## Roast

1. Take the chicken out an hour before cooking. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Rub the butter under and over the skin. Put the lemon and thyme in the cavity.
3. Roast 60-75 minutes, until the thickest part of the thigh reads 165°F and the
   juices run clear.
4. Rest 20 minutes before carving. This is not optional.

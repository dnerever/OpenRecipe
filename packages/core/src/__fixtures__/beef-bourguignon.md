## Brown everything

1. Render the lardons in a dutch oven, then lift them out.
2. Pat the beef dry and brown it hard in batches. Crowding the pan steams it.
3. Soften the carrot and onion in the fat, add the garlic and tomato paste, and
   cook until the paste darkens.

## Braise

1. Return the beef and lardons. Dust with flour and toss to coat.
2. Add the wine and stock, thyme and bay. Bring to a bare simmer.
3. Cover and cook at 325°F for 3 hours, until a fork slides in without
   resistance.

## Garnish

1. Brown the pearl onions and mushrooms separately in butter.
2. Fold them in for the last 20 minutes so they keep their shape.
3. Skim the fat, check the salt, and rest it off the heat for 20 minutes before
   serving.

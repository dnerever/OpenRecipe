Heat the oven to 300°F.

Toss everything except the cherries and egg whites together. Whip the whites to
soft peaks and fold them through — this is what makes clusters.

Press hard into a lined sheet pan in an even slab. Bake 45 minutes, rotating
once, without stirring.

Cool completely in the pan before breaking it up. Fold in the cherries.

Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then freeze it for an hour. Frozen
grains shatter and break down faster.

Bring the stock to a boil, add the rice, and drop to the barest simmer. Stir
every 15 minutes for 90 minutes, until the grains have dissolved into cream.

Marinate the pork in the soy sauce and a little white pepper. Slide it in during
the last five minutes; it will cook through in the residual heat.

Serve with the century egg, ginger and spring onion piled on top.

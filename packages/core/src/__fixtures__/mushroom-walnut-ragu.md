## Soffritto

Sweat the onion, carrot and celery in the olive oil for 20 minutes, until sweet
and completely soft. Do not colour it.

## Brown the mushrooms

Raise the heat and add the minced cremini and chopped porcini. Cook until every
drop of water has driven off and they start to fry rather than steam — 20
minutes at least. This is the step people cut short, and it is the one that
decides whether the ragù tastes of anything.

Stir in the walnuts and tomato paste and cook two minutes more.

## Cream

Pour in the oat cream and simmer until it has almost entirely disappeared into
the mushrooms.

## Wine, then tomato

Add the wine and reduce it away. Only then add the tomato, stock, porcini
soaking liquid and nutmeg.

## The long part

Barely simmer, uncovered, for 3 hours, topping up with water if it tightens too
far. It is done when the oil separates at the edges and the mixture holds its
shape on a spoon.

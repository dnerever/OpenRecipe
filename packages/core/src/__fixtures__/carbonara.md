  - { qty: 1, unit: tbsp, item: olive oil }
  - { qty: 200, unit: g, item: silken tofu }
  - { qty: 40, unit: g, item: cashews, note: "soaked 20 minutes in boiling water" }
  - { qty: 25, unit: g, item: nutritional yeast }
  - { qty: 0.5, unit: tsp, item: kala namak, note: "black salt, for the egg note" }
  - { qty: null, item: black pepper, note: "coarsely cracked, to taste" }

1. Fry the smoked tofu in the olive oil over medium heat until the edges are
   crisp and browned, about 8 minutes. Kill the heat and leave it in the pan.
2. Blend the silken tofu, drained cashews, nutritional yeast and kala namak
   until completely smooth. It should pour like thick cream.
3. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until just shy of al dente. Reserve a
   mug of the water.
4. Toss the pasta into the tofu pan off the heat, add a splash of pasta water,
   then the blended sauce, stirring constantly.
5. Return to the barest heat for a few seconds at a time until the sauce is
   glossy and clings to the strands. Loosen with pasta water as needed, and
   grind over far more black pepper than feels sensible.

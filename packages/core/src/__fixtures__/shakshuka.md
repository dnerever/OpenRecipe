- Soften the onion and peppers in the oil over medium heat for 15 minutes. Take
  your time here; this is most of the flavour.
- Add the garlic and spices and fry until fragrant.
- Add the tomatoes, crush them with a spoon, and simmer 15 minutes until thick
  enough to hold a channel.
- Make six wells and spoon in rough dollops of silken tofu. Scatter the kala
  namak over them, cover, and warm through for 5 minutes — long enough to heat,
  not so long that they collapse.
- Crumble over the tofu feta and coriander.

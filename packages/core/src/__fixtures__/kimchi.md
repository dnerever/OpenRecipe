## Salt the cabbage

Quarter the cabbages lengthwise and salt between every leaf. Rest 2 hours,
turning occasionally, until the stems bend without snapping. Rinse three times
and drain an hour.

## Porridge and paste

Whisk the rice flour into 250ml water and simmer until it thickens, then cool.
Blend it with the gochugaru, garlic, ginger and fish sauce.

## Combine and ferment

Toss the cabbage, daikon and spring onions through the paste, wearing gloves.
Pack into jars, pressing out air, leaving 3cm of headroom.

Leave at room temperature 2-3 days, burping daily, until it tastes pleasantly
sour. Refrigerate.

## The day before

Dissolve the salt in the water and submerge the whole head, weighing it down
with a plate. Brine 24 hours in the fridge. This seasons it right through, which
oil on the outside never does.

## Roast

1. Drain the cauliflower and pat it dry. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Whisk the oil, miso, lemon zest and garlic into a loose paste and rub it into
   every crevice, leaves included.
3. Roast 60-75 minutes, until a knife slides into the core with no resistance and
   the outer leaves are blackened and brittle.
4. Rest 10 minutes, scatter the thyme, and carve it in wedges at the table.

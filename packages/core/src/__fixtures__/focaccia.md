## Schedule

```
20:00  mix dough
20:30  fold
21:00  fold, then refrigerate
08:00  transfer to oiled tray
12:00  dimple and bake
```

## Method

Whisk the yeast and salt into the flour, add the water, and mix to a wet shaggy
dough. Fold twice, half an hour apart, then cover and refrigerate overnight.

In the morning, pour the dough into a generously oiled tray and gently stretch it
toward the corners. It will resist; let it relax 20 minutes and try again.

Prove 3-4 hours at room temperature until visibly puffed and bubbled.

Dimple hard with oiled fingers, right down to the pan. Scatter flaky salt and
rosemary. Bake at 450°F for 22-25 minutes until deep gold underneath.
